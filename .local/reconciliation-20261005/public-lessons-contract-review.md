# Public lessons contract — planning consolidation

The agreed definition and common examples are consolidated in the maintained
[public lessons contract](../../docs/contracts/public-lessons.md).

The [participant/reconciliation planning pass](participant-reconciliation-plan.md)
records the source validation, participant outputs, actual handoff/review path,
corrected evolution overreach and post-impact coordinating outcome.

The redundant blanket approval request is withdrawn. Earlier agreements remain
fixed. The maintained record is local and unpublished; both future assignments
will require its exact published commit-bound reference. Ticket breakdown,
commit/push and recipient release retain their existing checkpoints. No sibling
implementation or deployment is claimed by this consolidation.
