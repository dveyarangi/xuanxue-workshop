operator-agreed acceptance from agent additions before aligning the remaining
criteria. Fresh discovery returned originals 11, 12, 13, 15, 16 and 17. Their current

acceptance-authority audit, not a new implementation/runtime verification.

The [acceptance authority](../agent-contract.md#acceptance-authority) governs the
assessment. GitHub account names do not establish the speaking operator or approval:
the same account publishes both recipient reports and Workshop-agent reviews.
An individually recorded decision and recorded acceptance of a complete package
are distinguished below. An agent-authored detail inside a subsequently accepted
package is not classified as an unauthorized addition. Absence of a separate
approval trace is uncertainty, not proof that a requirement was never approved.
