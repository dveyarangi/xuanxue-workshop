## Accepted direction — 2026-10-12

The user asked how a person signed in at xuanxue.su can use wiki.xuanxue.su under
the same account despite separate frontends/backends. After reviewing the
shared-cookie alternative, its common lifetime/logout and absence of wiki
reader credential storage, the user replied "думаю да". The durable home is the
[accepted shared browser session](../../contracts/web-wiki-session.md#accepted-shared-browser-session).

Cabinet retains account/session authority; wiki uses the existing session cookie
with Domain=xuanxue.su, verified by Cabinet for current protected reads. Every
valid Cabinet-authenticated reader gets the same content. The browser namespace
includes the domain and its subdomains as one trusted session namespace. Lifetime,
renewal and browser logout are common. Wiki persists no reader token, local
session or account replica. Native bearer grants and other browser installations
retain their scope. Recipient implementation and deployed proof are not established.

## Cabinet-site logout simplification — 2026-10-13

Cabinet logout. No accepted requirement needs a wiki-side logout operation.

## Delivery breakdown assessment — 2026-10-13

### Impact

The proposed `web-wiki-cabinet-session` scope is one complete browser outcome:
Cabinet sign-in grants web-wiki reading, with automatic original-page return,
common renewal and Cabinet-site logout. Cabinet owns cookie issuance, current
account validation and browser navigation; Daychi owns protected web content
and the request-time consumer check. Workshop coordinates their shared contract
and joint acceptance. The [accepted contract](../../contracts/web-wiki-session.md)
already specifies these obligations; this assessment changes no accepted interface.
The candidate is HITL because the access cutover and deployed proof require
controlled environments and operator participation. No ticket has been minted
from this presentation.

### Hidden edges

The whole outcome includes legacy host-only cookie migration, rejection of
duplicate session values, rolling-cookie delivery, per-tab return compatible with
native continuations, and refusal versus temporary unavailability. Closing
anonymous access includes reader APIs, directly exposed assets and controlled
old caches. The existing [structured article coordination issue](https://github.com/dveyarangi/xuanxue-workshop/issues/9)
preserves active public access: the new web-reader cutover therefore needs a
coordinated amendment, not a retrospective failure of earlier article work.
The isolated browser witnesses establish transport behavior only; acceptance
needs the implemented Cabinet/Daychi path on controlled deployed origins.

### Leave alone

Cabinet remains the account owner; wiki gains no reader credential or account
replica. Native account-session work, native private-content access, editorial
authority, owner/import operations, article formats, schedules and reminders
keep their accepted scopes. Existing native assignments do not cover this web
outcome. No gateway, client secret or independent wiki session is needed.

### Recommendation

Proceed with one end-to-end coordination ticket rather than provider-only and
consumer-only tickets. Its acceptance covers signed-in reading, original-page
return, shared renewal/logout, failure behavior and coordinated removal of
anonymous web reading. No other ticket must finish before the two projects can
prepare their implementations against the published immutable contract. Joint
deployed proof depends on compatible running implementations and controlled
accounts/origins. Minting this candidate awaits the user's breakdown approval;
recipient assignments and contract publication have not occurred in this pass.
