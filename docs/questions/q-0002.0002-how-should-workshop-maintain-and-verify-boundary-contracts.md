and boundary evidence with Workshop documentation, refresh that documentation
and derive short project boundary summaries, then issue addressed work when a
changed summary requires project action. The
[/reconcile skill](../../.agents/skills/reconcile/SKILL.md) now implements that
procedure. It is declared and connected to the /maintain skill through local rules;

and remaining limitations. This does not establish a completed reconciliation
or replace the separate startup-review work.

and recipient conventions must be read through pointers to their authoritative
documents, not repeated in the skill. Installation into maintenance belongs to
