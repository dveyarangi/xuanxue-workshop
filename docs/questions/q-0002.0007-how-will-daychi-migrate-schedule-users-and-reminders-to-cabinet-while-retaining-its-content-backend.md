## Accepted direction

schedule access, reminder choices and delivery, skip removal and rationale,
and content-admission decision. Do not reopen them as feature proposals.

The [current source inventory](../current-system.md) distinguishes implemented
capabilities from the target. The [required delta](../migration-changes.md)
holds compatibility gaps, review scenarios and the unapproved contribution split.
The [first-stage goal](../stage-1.md) limits this to initial migration and agent
integration under separate operators.

## Open work

transition, local-choice/account association, lead times, saved skip exceptions,
and failure behavior. Authentication protocol and lifecycle have their own
[question](q-0002.0008.0001-how-will-daychi-clients-obtain-and-present-cabinet-credentials.md).
Reuse the existing refresh and local delivery flow; adding native push or new
background polling is outside scope.

## Refuted proposals

The assistant proposed native server push as the primary Daychi delivery channel;
the user rejected the extra mechanism on 2026-10-04. The assistant also framed
reminders without sign-in as a new product choice. Source inspection established
that the behavior already exists; that question was closed as moot. These errors
must not become work items during contribution planning.
