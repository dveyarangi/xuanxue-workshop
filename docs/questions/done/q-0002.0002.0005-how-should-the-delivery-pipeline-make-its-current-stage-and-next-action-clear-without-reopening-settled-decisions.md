## Distinct causes

1. **A restrictive rule hides a delivery dependency.** AGENTS.md Autonomy/push
   reserves asking for or even mentioning push to /conclude. The conclude skill
   repeats that restriction. The local `push=ask` switch correctly preserves a
   separate approval, but the timing restriction prevents requesting it when
   publication is necessary for an in-progress recipient handoff.
2. **Settled decisions and the pending action were not carried forward clearly.**
   The entry contract prohibits reopening accepted decisions without new evidence;
   L10 keeps an accepted decision fixed while resolving its consequences.
   Coordinate's continuation section retains the selected step under unchanged
   circumstances. The earlier diagnosis incorrectly treated repeated verification
   as the central failure. A check may be repeated without asking the operator to
   decide an already settled question again.
3. **The reply hid the concrete action.** The assistant's phrase "обеспечить
   доступную закреплённую ревизию" omitted both the responsible actor and the
   immediate GitHub action/permission. No instruction required that wording.

Coordinate's Outputs section reports outcome and remaining work with links, but
does not explicitly require the reply to identify the current stage, next action,
its owner and any condition for proceeding. This is the specific communication
gap under consideration, rather than a missing ban on repeated checks.

The homeHiddenTiles source change did justify the affected independent re-read.
Preparing a partial commit from a dirty tree also justified checking its actual
contents and links. Removing those checks would weaken different obligations;
the user's correction does not call for prohibiting other repeated checks.

The repeated continuation failure already has a
[register entry](../../rule-failures.md#2026-10-04--alignment-stopped-after-answering-a-clarification).
Its general reminder was refused as redundant. Another generalized continuation
rule or a separate per-check ledger is not the proposed repair.

## Cases that distinguish the intended behavior

| Situation | Expected behavior |
|---|---|
| Reviewed local commit is necessary for recipient implementation and push permission is absent | Name the commit and GitHub action, ask for push permission then, and preserve completed review |
| User asks what the accepted contract or next step means; governing decisions are unchanged | Clarify the current stage and next action, retain accepted decisions and resume authorized work; verification may be repeated |
| A source change adds a shared DTO field | Reconcile the field and re-read affected obligations independently |
| Only publication links or recipient labels change | Check publication and show the resulting handoff; retain the accepted protocol unless evidence contradicts it |
| A check is repeated and still supports the accepted decision | The decision stays settled; no new operator decision is needed |
| Changed evidence undermines an accepted decision | Identify the changed evidence and the affected decision before returning that decision to alignment |
