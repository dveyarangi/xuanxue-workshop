# analyse — scoped project evidence with explicit limits

- **instruction** `.agents/skills/analyse/SKILL.md`
- **state** installed
- **record of** what must always hold

## How it works

Analysis owns the investigation subjects and provenance of observations. It
inspects available project sources before requesting evidence and returns changed
facts or explicit unknowns. It has no authority to amend shared promises.
A delegated repository investigation has the same scope and output obligations.

## What it produces, and who reads it

Scoped observations update the configured existing factual home or return to
the caller; no new record grammar or index is introduced. Reconciliation, issuing
and review read their source revisions, claim coverage and unknowns. Existing
project record ownership and maintenance remain unchanged.

## Not yet at the shape

Mechanical checks establish declaration, binding and reference integrity, not
semantic correctness of observations, agreements or assignments. Explicit evidence
limits and independent output grading bound those conclusions.

## What retires this

Retire when its responsibility is removed or an accepted replacement assumes its
outputs and readers. Retract its installed rules and update dependents first.

## What would show it working, graded by someone who did not build it

A reader checks a source-observed route against an independently checked
deployment, an inaccessible repository and mixed-revision evidence. Analysis must
not equate source with deployment, refresh old facts by changing a heading, or
delegate investigation it can perform. A non-boundary change need not cause work.

The declaration and installation checkers must reject missing instructions and
missing or changed installed rules, and accept restored fixtures. Maintenance uses
the coordinator's shared binding without an additional per-stage report.
