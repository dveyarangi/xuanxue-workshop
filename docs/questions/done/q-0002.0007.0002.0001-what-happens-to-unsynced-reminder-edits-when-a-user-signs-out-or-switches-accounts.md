Opened during migration alignment on 2026-10-04. The
[offline-editing contract](../../boundaries.md#accepted-offline-reminder-editing)
requires preserving pending reminder and lead-time edits through refreshes,
connection failures and retries. Cabinet owns account state. The explicit
sign-out case was unresolved when this question opened; its resolution follows.

Scenario: a user changes a reminder while offline, signs out before synchronization,
and signs in with another Cabinet account. The pending change belongs to the
original account; replaying it into the new account would change the wrong
account. This scenario exposed retention versus deliberate discard on sign-out.

Source check: Daychi schedule preferences currently use device-wide AsyncStorage
keys, while `logoutAccess` in `features/access/session.ts` clears its credential.
Neither is an existing Cabinet account-bound offline synchronization contract.
Cabinet web logout clears its session, but its current notification settings do
not provide a native pending-edit queue. No governing decision for this case was
found in the boundary, migration, credential or synchronization records.

The recommendation presented was to retain unsynced reminder and lead-time edits bound
only to the original Cabinet account, suspend their synchronization while signed
out, and resume only after authenticating as that same account. Switching accounts
must not replay the earlier edits into the newly signed-in account. This preserves
intent but retains account-related data on the device after sign-out.

Alternative: discard unsynced edits on explicit sign-out. This simplifies local
cleanup and keeps less account data on the device, but loses changes made offline
that Cabinet has not received. Temporary connection failures remain subject to
the accepted preservation rule under either choice.

This question concerns pending account writes; it does not prescribe frontend
presentation, local storage encoding or token lifecycle. The implementing projects
must propose and verify the corresponding account-isolation contract.

## Accepted discard on explicit sign-out — 2026-10-04

The user chose: "discard it". Explicit sign-out discards unsynced reminder and
lead-time edits. They are not replayed after later sign-in or into another
account; Cabinet's already-accepted state is unaffected. Temporary connection
failures still preserve pending edits. The
[offline-editing contract](../../boundaries.md#accepted-offline-reminder-editing)
owns this rule. Storage cleanup, in-flight synchronization handling and executable
checks are project proposals; no frontend warning or interaction is prescribed.
