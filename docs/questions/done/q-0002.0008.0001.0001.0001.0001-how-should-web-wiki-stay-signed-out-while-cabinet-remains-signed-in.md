## Accepted constraints

The [accepted web-wiki handoff](../../boundaries.md#accepted-web-wiki-sign-in-handoff)
reuses Cabinet's browser sign-in and creates a local wiki session. The
[accepted sign-out scope](../../boundaries.md#accepted-sign-out-scope)
ends authenticated use in the calling client while other clients remain signed in.

## Raised consequence — 2026-10-12

If every missing wiki session immediately initiates the accepted handoff, a wiki
sign-out while Cabinet remains signed in can immediately create another wiki
session. The current-client exit and ordinary automatic-entry behavior need a
consistent distinction. The proposal was to suppress automatic re-entry after
explicit wiki sign-out until deliberate sign-in, while preserving Cabinet's session.

## Accepted answer — the user, 2026-10-12

The user replied "хорошо" to that proposal. Wiki remains unauthenticated after
explicit exit until the reader deliberately starts sign-in again; Cabinet remains
signed in and can supply the new handoff without another password when eligible.
The durable answer is
[accepted web-wiki explicit sign-out](../../boundaries.md#accepted-web-wiki-explicit-sign-out).
Storage, expiry, revocation and failure semantics belong to the remaining web
profile; this answer fixes the observable exit/re-entry policy, not its implementation.
